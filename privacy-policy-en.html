1. Introduction

QuitterPal ("we", "us", or "our") respects and protects the privacy of all users. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information. By using our app, you agree to the terms of this policy.

2. Information We Collect

When you use our app, we may collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Email address, custom ID, display name, password (stored as SHA256 hash)
  • Device Information: Device registration ID (for push notifications)
  • Usage Data: App blocking statistics, helper relationships, guardian request records
  • Subscription Info: Trial start time, payment status (if applicable)
  • Google Login Info: Google ID (only if you choose Google Sign-In)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing your account
  • Providing core app features (addiction guardian, helper relationships, blocking statistics)
  • Sending push notifications (JPush / Firebase Cloud Messaging)
  • Sending password reset emails (via Resend)
  • Processing in-app subscriptions and payments (if applicable)
  • Improving app experience and functionality

4. Third-Party Services

We may use the following third-party services, which may collect and process some of your data:

  • Firebase (Google): For overseas push notifications (FCM) and analytics
  • JPush (Aurora Mobile): For domestic push notifications
  • Resend: For sending password reset emails
  • Google Sign-In: For Google account login (optional)

These third-party services are subject to their respective privacy policies.

5. Data Security

  • Your password is encrypted using SHA256 hashing and stored securely. We cannot view your plaintext password.
  • Data transmission uses HTTPS encryption.
  • Databases are deployed on secure cloud servers with regular backups.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data for as long as you use the app. You may delete your account and related data at any time through the app's features. After deletion, your personal information will be removed from our servers within a reasonable time.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and view your personal information
  • Modify your account information (custom ID, display name, etc.)
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Exit helper relationships or pause guardian features

8. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we may have collected information from a child, please contact us to have it removed.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated policies will be posted within the app and on this page. Users will be notified of significant changes in advance.
