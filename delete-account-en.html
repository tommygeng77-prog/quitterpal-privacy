Delete Your QuitterPal Account

We respect your right to control your personal data. You can request account deletion at any time.

How to Delete Your Account

Method 1: Email Request (Recommended)
Send an email to tommygeng77@gmail.com with the subject "Delete Account Request".
Include your registered email address in the body.
Method 2: In-App Request
Go to Account → Settings → Delete Account (feature coming soon).

What Data Will Be Deleted

  • Account information (email, custom ID, password hash)
  • Helper relationships and pairing history
  • Guard statistics and app block lists
  • Subscription/trial records
  • Push notification registration IDs

Data Retention

After deletion, your data will be permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

For legal or security purposes, we may retain minimal logs (such as IP addresses and timestamps) for up to 90 days before automatic purging.

Important Notes

  • Account deletion is irreversible.
  • Active subscriptions should be canceled separately through Google Play.
  • If you have paired helpers, the relationships will be automatically unbound.
